For You

To our incredible friend,

Before anything else, you are our person. The one who shows up without being asked, who listens like it matters, who makes hard days lighter just by being in the room. Being your friend has been one of the easiest, best parts of our lives — you love loudly, loyally, and completely, and we are so lucky to be on the receiving end of that.

And then there's who you are in that pool. There's a special kind of person who can look at the water and see poetry — who trains before the sun's fully up, who pushes her swimmers not because she has to, but because she believes, completely, that those girls are capable of something extraordinary. That's you too.

We've watched you pour everything into this sport — the discipline, the patience, the sheer stubborn determination that doesn't quit even on the hard days. And it shows. Not just in the medals, but in the way your team believes in themselves now. Third place at state isn't just a trophy — it's proof of every early practice, every correction, every "one more time," every ounce of love you've poured into them.

We are so, so proud of you — for who you are, and for everything you've built.

And this isn't the finish line. This is just the beginning.

We're not done yet.
